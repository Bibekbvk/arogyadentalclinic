Preventive Care

Scaling & Polishing (Teeth Cleaning)

Gentle ultrasonic removal of stubborn tartar, dental plaque, and tea/coffee stains. Prevents gum disease, bleeding gums, and bad breath.

  • Painless ultrasonic technology
  • Enamel-safe stain removal
  • Instant freshness & shine

Restorative Care

Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

Save severely infected or decayed natural teeth without extraction. Modern rotary endodontics ensure comfortable and virtually painless treatment.

  • Rapid severe pain relief
  • Saves natural teeth longevity
  • Precision rotary instrumentation
Single/Multiple visits
Oral Surgery

Tooth Extractions & Impactions

Safe removal of non-restorable teeth, broken roots, and impacted third molars (wisdom teeth) with gentle localized anesthesia and rapid recovery.

  • Detailed post-care guidelines
Emergency care ready
